Meu Primeiro Devocional / My First Devotional

Bible Verses for Little Hearts

Little hearts, big discoveries about God's love!

Every day is a new opportunity to teach children about faith, love, gratitude, and kindness. This bilingual Portuguese–English Christian devotional introduces little ones to God's Word through gentle illustrations, short Bible-inspired messages, and simple prayers.

Each devotional includes a Bible verse in Portuguese and English, an easy-to-understand reflection, and a short prayer. Perfect for family reading, bedtime, or daily devotional moments.

More than a book, it's an invitation to nurture faith from childhood and create memories that stay in the heart.
