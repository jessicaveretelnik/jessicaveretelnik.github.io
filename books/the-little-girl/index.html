The Little Girl / Маленька дівчинка

A dream come true. A war that changes everything. A story of faith and hope. When a little Ukrainian girl travels to America with her grandmother, her biggest dream finally comes true. But when war breaks out in Ukraine, her world changes overnight. Far from home and separated from her loved ones, she discovers that even in the darkest moments, God's love and hope remain. Inspired by a true story, this heartfelt bilingual book in English and Ukrainian reminds children of the power of prayer, the strength of family, and the hope of being reunited.
