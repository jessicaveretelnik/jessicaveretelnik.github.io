The Little Girl / Маленька дівчинка

A dream come true. A war that changes everything. A story of faith and hope. A little Ukrainian girl dreams of visiting America. When her grandmother surprises her with a special trip, she finally gets to experience the adventure she has imagined for so long. Together, they discover new places and make unforgettable memories. But while they are far from home, war breaks out in Ukraine, and everything changes. Separated from the people she loves, the little girl faces uncertainty and longs for the day her family can be together again. Through prayer, love, and faith, she finds comfort in the hope that God remains near, even in the hardest moments. Inspired by a true story, this tender bilingual book in English and Ukrainian invites children and families to reflect on courage, compassion, peace, and the power of hope.
